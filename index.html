Pixel Putt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pixel Putt does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

The golfer names you type, your settings, and your Best Golfers scores are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Nearby play

When you choose Nearby, Pixel Putt uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi to find other iPhones close by. Your golfer name, ball colour and your shots are sent directly to the phones in your game, and nowhere else. Nothing goes over the internet. iOS asks for local network permission the first time; it is used only to find nearby players.

Children

Pixel Putt is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app is.
